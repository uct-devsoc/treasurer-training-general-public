# Who to contact

Go in this order. Most problems are solved at the first step.

## Before anything else: the previous treasurer

For anything this site does not answer, ask the person who did the job before you. Your society's handover document should have their details.

## The fund holder mailbox

**societies.fundholder@uct.ac.za**

A shared Department of Student Affairs mailbox. The fund holder signs every form, but a small team reads the mailbox and checks forms on the fund holder's behalf, so the person replying to you may not be the fund holder. Do not email the fund holder's personal address; a form sent there sits outside the queue. Contact the mailbox for:

- submitting an MM010 (the Word document and the quote as a named PDF)
- starting a sponsor invoice
- registering a new sponsor company (SD004)
- questions about whether a spend is allowed

The mailbox serves every society at UCT. Send complete paperwork with everything they need in one email, and expect a reply to take up to a week. Nobody there will remind you about anything. If it has been longer than a week, follow up in the same chain, and if that goes unanswered, ask Student Treasury.

## Student Treasury

Room 5.10, Steve Biko Building, North Lane, Upper Campus. Contact them for:

- the current balance of the fund (in person)
- the status of a submitted MM010 or purchase order
- how a vendor registers with UCT
- a copy of a purchase order you did not receive
- your society's fund, cost centre and GL numbers if your handover did not include them
- anything about the MM010 that the fund holder mailbox cannot answer

Treasury emails the PO back to you and expects the invoice and delivery confirmation from you afterwards, in the same chain. They keep your phone number on the form for their records but do not phone, and they do not chase treasurers: every follow-up is yours to send.

## UCT Finance: Creditors and Debtors

You will rarely contact these directly, but you should know they exist.

- **Creditors** (fnd-creditors@uct.ac.za) pays vendors once Treasury passes the invoice on. If a vendor says they have not been paid, first check that you sent the invoice to Treasury with the PO number on it and a delivery confirmation, then follow up in that chain.
- **Debtors** issues invoices to sponsors and registers new customers from the SD004. You reach them through the fund holder mailbox, not directly.

## UCT forms

All the forms mentioned on this site are on [forms.uct.ac.za](https://forms.uct.ac.za/). Download the current version every time.
