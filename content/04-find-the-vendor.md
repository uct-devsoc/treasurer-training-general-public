# Find the vendor

UCT can only pay a supplier that is already registered on SAP as a vendor. Every registered vendor has a six-digit vendor number, and the MM010 needs both the vendor's registered name and that number.

Treasury keeps the full list as a large spreadsheet. The search below covers every vendor on it. Search by name, by town, or by number, and use the categories to narrow the list for things like catering and printing.

<div id="vendor-search"></div>

## What to copy onto the form

Copy the **registered name** exactly as it appears here, not the name on the vendor's letterhead. A registered name is often a legal name plus a trading name, for example "Dokrek Beleggings T/A Debonairs Pizza Rondebosch". The form accepts the trading-name part, but the full registered name is the safer choice. Copy the **vendor number** as well.

## If the vendor is not here

They are not registered, and UCT cannot pay them. You have two options.

1. **Choose a registered vendor instead.** For common things (food, printing, transport) the categories above list vendors that other societies have used.
2. **Ask the vendor to register.** Student Treasury can tell you how. It takes weeks, needs the vendor to supply company and banking documents, and is only worth it for a supplier you will use repeatedly.

Do not submit an MM010 for an unregistered vendor. It will come back, and it will not make the registration go any faster.
