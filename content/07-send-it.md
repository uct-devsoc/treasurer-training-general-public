# Send it, and what happens next

The form is only the start. Here is the whole chain, who does each step, and where it tends to stall.

:::chain
You | Email the MM010 (as a Word document) and the quote (as a PDF named with your society and the date) to the fund holder mailbox, **societies.fundholder@uct.ac.za**. One email, both attachments, a one-line message saying what it is for and the VAT-exclusive amount.
Fund holder team | Checks the form. Several people work in that mailbox and any of them may check it; the signature on the form is always the fund holder's. If anything is wrong or missing the form comes back, and the clock restarts. Allow up to a week.
Student Treasury | Receives the signed form, checks it, and creates a purchase order (PO) in SAP. The PO is a one-page PDF with a seven-digit order number.
Student Treasury | Emails the PO back to **you**, in the same email chain.
You | Forward the PO to the vendor. Treasury does not contact the vendor; until you send it, the vendor has nothing. Do this in a separate email chain with the vendor.
Vendor | Delivers on the date in the quote, then sends you the invoice, with the PO number on it.
You | Reply in the Treasury email chain: attach the invoice and confirm in writing that the goods were delivered.
UCT Creditors | Pays the vendor. If the vendor says they have not been paid, follow up in the Treasury chain.
:::

## Two email chains, kept separate

Everything with the fund holder and Treasury lives in one chain: the form, the signed form, the PO, the invoice, the delivery confirmation. Everything with the vendor lives in another: the quote, the PO you forward, the invoice they send you. Do not mix them. Treasury needs to be able to scroll one thread and see the whole order.

## Nobody chases you

The fund holder mailbox will not remind you that a form is sitting there with a mistake. Treasury will not phone. If you have heard nothing from the fund holder mailbox in a week, follow up in the same chain. If that goes unanswered, ask Student Treasury. If the vendor has not invoiced you after delivery, ask the vendor; an unpaid vendor will not quote you again.

## The email to the fund holder

Something like this is enough.

> Hi, please find attached the MM010 (Word) and the quote for lunch for our Git workshop on 12 March, R2 130.43 excluding VAT, vendor Suraya's Restaurant Coffee Shop (204246). Could you sign and forward to Student Treasury? Thank you.

Attach both files. Do not paste your society's fund, cost centre or GL numbers into the email body; they are on the form.

## Keep the paperwork

For every order, keep the quote, the MM010 you sent, the PO and the invoice together in one folder. These are the records the next treasurer will need, and the ones you will need for your report at the AGM.
