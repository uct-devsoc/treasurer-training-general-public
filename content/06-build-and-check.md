# Build and check your MM010

Fill in the form below exactly as you would fill in the real one. As you type, the panel beside the form (below it on a phone) shows the form the way Treasury will see it and runs every check from the previous page. Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere; close the page and it is gone.

Use it to practise, or to check a real order before you type it into the Word template.
