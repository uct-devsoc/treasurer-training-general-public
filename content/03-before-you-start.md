# Before you start

Do not open the form until you have the five things below. A form started without them is a form that comes back.

## 1. A quote, not a screenshot of a menu

A dated quotation on the vendor's letterhead, made out to the **University of Cape Town**, with a quote number, line items, the VAT-exclusive subtotal, the VAT and the total. Ask for it in that form; most vendors that work with UCT know the drill. If a vendor only gives VAT-inclusive prices, the page on fields explains how to convert them.

Save the quote as a PDF named with your society and the date, for example `DevSoc_20_Sep_2026.pdf`. That is the file you will attach to the form.

## 2. The vendor number

From the vendor search on the next page. If the vendor is not there, they are not registered and UCT cannot pay them. Either pick a registered vendor or ask Student Treasury how the vendor can register, which takes weeks. Do not fill in an MM010 for an unregistered vendor.

## 3. The current MM010 template

Download it fresh from [forms.uct.ac.za](https://forms.uct.ac.za/) every time. The wording on the form changes a few times a year, and Treasury rejects old versions. It is a Word document, and it must stay a Word document.

:::watch
Treasury only accepts the MM010 as a **Word document**, not a PDF. This changed in August 2026 after PDFs arrived that could not be edited. Send the .docx.
:::

## 4. Your society's three numbers

The fund number, cost centre and GL account number, from your handover or from Student Treasury. You will type them into every item row on the form.

## 5. Enough money in the fund

Check the balance in person if you are not sure. A form for more than the fund holds will not be approved.

## How much time to allow

On correctly completed forms, the purchase order has been created four to five days after the MM010 was submitted. A form that comes back for corrections adds a week or more, mostly waiting for the fund holder again. Submit at least **two weeks** before you need the goods, and earlier for anything large.

## Two quotes, three quotes

The form asks you to confirm that the correct vendor selection procedure was followed. The procedure depends on the size of the purchase. Below R100 000, one written quote is enough. Above R100 000 UCT needs two written quotes, and above R500 000 it needs three. Societies almost never buy anything near R100 000, so in practice you tick YES and attach your one quote.
