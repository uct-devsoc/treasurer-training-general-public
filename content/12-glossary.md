# Glossary

**AGM.** Annual General Meeting. Where the treasurer's report is presented and the next exec is elected.

**Cost centre.** The organisational unit in UCT's accounting system that your society's fund belongs to. One of the three society numbers.

**Creditors.** The part of UCT Finance that pays suppliers. Treasury passes the vendor's invoice to Creditors with the PO number on it.

**Debtors.** The part of UCT Finance that sends invoices to organisations that owe UCT money, such as sponsors, and registers them as customers.

**DSA.** Department of Student Affairs. Societies sit under the DSA, and "Department of Student Affairs" is what goes in the Department field on the MM010.

**Fund.** Your society's pot of money inside UCT's accounting system. There is no bank account.

**Fund holder.** The DSA staff member responsible for society funds, who signs every MM010 and authorises every sponsor invoice. Reached through the shared mailbox societies.fundholder@uct.ac.za.

**Fund number.** The number of your society's fund. One of the three society numbers.

**GL account number.** General ledger account number. Classifies the type of transaction in UCT's books. One of the three society numbers.

**MM010.** UCT's purchase order request form. The form you fill in whenever your society wants to buy something. A Word document downloaded from forms.uct.ac.za.

**PO, purchase order.** UCT's formal commitment to a vendor to pay for specified goods. Created by Student Treasury in SAP from an approved MM010. Has a seven-digit number that must appear on the vendor's invoice.

**Quote, quotation.** A dated document from a vendor stating what they will supply and at what price, made out to the University of Cape Town. Required for every MM010.

**Registered vendor.** A supplier that UCT has set up on SAP and is allowed to pay. Has a vendor number. UCT cannot pay anyone else.

**SAP.** The accounting system UCT runs on. You never touch it; Treasury and Finance do.

**SD004.** UCT's customer application form. Registers an outside organisation so Debtors can invoice it. Needed once for each new sponsor that has never dealt with UCT.

**Student Treasury.** The office in the DSA that processes society purchases and tells you the balance. Room 5.10, Steve Biko Building.

**VAT-exclusive, VAT-inclusive.** A VAT-inclusive amount includes 15% value-added tax; a VAT-exclusive amount does not. The MM010 takes VAT-exclusive amounts. To get from inclusive to exclusive, divide by 1.15.

**Vendor number.** The six-digit number identifying a registered vendor. Found in the vendor search on this site. Required on every MM010.
