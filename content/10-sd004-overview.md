# What the SD004 is for

*This section is a placeholder. It shows where the SD004 walkthrough will sit and the shape it will take, so that the handbook can grow one form at a time. The content below is an outline, not yet a guide.*

The SD004 is UCT's **customer application form**. The MM010 is for money going out; the SD004 is part of money coming in. When a company owes your society money, most often a sponsor paying a partnership fee, UCT Debtors has to invoice that company. Debtors can only invoice a customer that exists in UCT's accounting system. If the company has never dealt with UCT before, the SD004 is how it gets registered.

## What this section will cover, when written

Each form section follows the same steps, so a treasurer who has done one form knows how to read the next.

1. **Before you start.** When an SD004 is needed and when it is not (a company that has paid UCT before is already a customer). The letter of liability, which comes first. What to collect from the company: registered name, registration number, VAT number, billing address, accounts contact.
2. **The form, field by field.** Every field, what goes in it, and which ones Debtors sends back.
3. **Build and check.** A practice form with live checks, like the MM010 tool.
4. **Send it, and what happens next.** Who the form goes to (the fund holder mailbox, not Debtors directly), who fills in which parts, how long registration takes, and how the invoice follows.
5. **Worked example.** A real registration, anonymised.
6. **Practice exercise.** A scenario with a model answer.

## What is already known

- The company, not the treasurer, supplies most of the information, and usually signs the form. The treasurer's job is to send it the form, chase it, and forward the completed form with supporting documents through the fund holder mailbox.
- Money a sponsor has promised is not money in the fund. It is only there when Student Treasury confirms it has landed.
- The current SD004 template is on [forms.uct.ac.za](https://forms.uct.ac.za/). As with the MM010, download it fresh each time.
