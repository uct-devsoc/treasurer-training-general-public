# The form, field by field

Open the current MM010 template next to this page. The form is one page: a block of order details at the top, a table of item details in the middle, an authorisation block, and a processing block that Treasury fills in. Every field is listed below, in the order it appears, with what goes in it and what has caused rejections.

The rule that covers everything: **every section must be completed.** Treasury rejects forms with empty cells, even cells that seem not to apply.

## Order details

**Requester: Name.** Your full name, as the treasurer.

**Tel.** Your mobile number, with the country code, for example +27 83 000 0000. Treasury keeps it for their records. They do not phone treasurers, so do not wait for a call.

**Department.** Always **Department of Student Affairs**. Not the society's name, not an academic department. Societies belong to the DSA in UCT's structure.

:::watch
Before August 2026, forms that put the society's name under Department and the vendor's legal name under Purchasing group were accepted. Since August 2026 they are not. Department is the DSA, Purchasing group is the society. A form filled the old way comes back.
:::

**Date.** The day you submit, as dd/mm/yyyy.

**Purchasing group.** The name of the society making the purchase, as it is registered with the DSA, for example "UCT Developer Society".

**Vendor name.** The vendor's name exactly as it appears in the vendor register, which is often a legal name plus a trading name. Copy it from the vendor search, not from the quote's letterhead.

**Vendor number.** The six-digit number from the vendor search. The form says "if known"; for you it is always known, because you will not submit a form without it.

## Item details

One row per item, or one row per kind of item. Both work. One society's approved forms include four rows for four kinds of pizza on one order, and a single row for "pizza" on another. Fewer rows means fewer chances to mistype, and a single row for the whole order at the quote's VAT-exclusive subtotal is often the cleanest.

**Item details.** The form asks for the quotation number and a short description in 40 characters. Put the quote number first, then what it is, then which event and when. For example: `Q#D06082026 pizza for AGM 25 Aug`. Treasury needs to see what the money is for and when, and rejects vague descriptions like "drinks" or "event costs". Descriptions a little over 40 characters have been accepted, so treat 40 as the target rather than a wall, but keep it tight.

**Qty.** How many. If you describe a category ("pizza for the AGM") rather than a single item, quantity is 1 and the unit amount is the whole category's cost.

**Unit net amount in original currency (excl. VAT).** The price of one unit **without VAT**. Take it from the quote's VAT-exclusive figures. If the quote only gives VAT-inclusive prices, divide by 1.15.

**Currency.** ZAR.

**Total ZAR amount (excl. VAT).** Quantity times unit amount, without VAT. The total of this column must equal the quote's VAT-exclusive subtotal exactly, to the cent.

:::watch
The single most common error on society forms is putting the VAT-inclusive total on the MM010. The form wants amounts **excluding** VAT. UCT adds VAT itself when it creates the purchase order, which is why the PO shows a bigger number than your form. If your form shows R4 345.00 and the quote's subtotal is R3 778.26, your form is wrong.
:::

**Fund number, Cost centre / Real internal order, GL account number.** Your society's three numbers, typed into every item row. The GL number is split into single-digit boxes on the form; type one digit per box.

**TOTAL.** The sum of the Total ZAR column. Must match the quote's VAT-exclusive subtotal.

## Authorisation

**Correct vendor selection procedure followed.** Tick **YES**. One quote is enough below R100 000.

**Conflict of interest.** Tick **NO**, and leave the details cell blank. A conflict of interest would be, for example, the vendor being a member of the exec or their family. If that is ever true, stop and talk to Student Treasury before submitting.

**Fund holder.** Leave the name, signature and date blank. The fund holder fills these in when signing. Do not type their name in.

**Above R100 000 and above R2 000 000.** Leave blank. Society purchases do not reach these.

## Processing details

Leave blank. Treasury fills in the PO number, value and date.

## Before you send: the checklist

- Downloaded today's template from forms.uct.ac.za.
- Saved as a Word document, named clearly, for example `DevSoc MM010 AGM 2026-08-11.docx`.
- Department is "Department of Student Affairs"; Purchasing group is your society's name.
- Vendor name and number copied from the vendor search.
- Every amount excludes VAT and the TOTAL equals the quote's subtotal.
- Description includes the quote number, the item, the event and the date.
- Fund number, cost centre and GL number on every row, checked twice.
- YES to vendor selection procedure, NO to conflict of interest.
- Fund holder block blank.
- Quote attached to the same email as a PDF named with the society and date.
