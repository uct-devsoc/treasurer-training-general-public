# A handbook for society treasurers

Every student society and structure under the Department of Student Affairs runs its money through the same small set of UCT finance forms. This site walks you through them, one form at a time, in the order you would actually do things: what to gather before you open the form, what goes in every field, what causes rejections, and what happens after you press send.

## Who this is for

Any treasurer of a UCT student society or other DSA structure. You do not need to have done this before. Nothing here is specific to one society; wherever your society's own details belong on a form, the site tells you where to get them.

## The forms

Each form has its own section in the contents, with numbered steps. Work through a section in order the first time; after that you will mostly come back for the field guide and the tools.

| Form | What it does | Section |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **MM010** | Purchase order request. Asks UCT to buy something from a registered vendor and pay them. The form you will use most. | Complete, with a vendor search and a build-and-check tool |
| **SD004** | Customer application. Registers an outside organisation, such as a sponsor, so UCT can invoice it. | Placeholder. The structure is there; the content is still to be written |

More forms are added as sections in the same shape. The reference pages at the bottom of the contents (who to contact, glossary) apply to all of them.

## Before you start on any form

Read **How the money works** first. It is short, and it explains why every form looks the way it does: a society has no bank account, only a fund inside UCT's accounting system, and every form is a way of asking UCT to move money in or out of it.

## The three things to remember

1. **Only registered vendors.** UCT can only pay a supplier that already has a UCT vendor number. Check before you ask for a quote.
2. **Amounts exclude VAT.** Every number on a purchase form is without VAT. Putting the VAT-inclusive total on the form is the single most common reason a form comes back.
3. **Nobody will chase you.** The fund holder and Student Treasury process what they receive. Every follow-up is yours to send.
